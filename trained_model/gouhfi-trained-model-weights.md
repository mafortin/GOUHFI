## Download Model Weights

The trained models for GOUHFI (3 models of ~7.4 GB each) are hosted on Zenodo:

[Download from Zenodo](https://zenodo.org/records/17920473)  
DOI: [10.5281/zenodo.17920473](https://zenodo.org/records/17920473)

You can download the `.zip` files and unzip it into the expected `trained_model/` directory in this repository.
